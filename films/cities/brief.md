# cities

## The request (verbatim)

An animated chart of the ten largest cities by population over the last century, with the source on screen. 30 seconds.

## Interpretation

**Goal:** the century as a race: ten bars trading places, a decade per beat, ending on today's
top ten with the source credited on screen.

**Explicit:** the ten largest cities BY POPULATION; over the LAST CENTURY (a century of data);
the source ON SCREEN; 30 seconds.

**Implied:** the chart technique (the grown capability — chart.json + the data/axis gates);
every figure a sourced fact (population numbers are world-claims, not math); a decade-per-beat
pace; the data from an open source with a snapshot.
