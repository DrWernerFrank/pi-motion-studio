# gps

## The request (verbatim)

A 60-second narrated explainer on how GPS knows where you are, with sources for every number.

## Interpretation

**Goal in one line:** in 60 seconds, show how a phone finds its spot from satellite signals — the
clock trick that turns time-of-flight into distance, and four satellites into a position.

**Explicit constraints:** 60 seconds; narrated; "sources for every number" (every figure said or
shown carries a source snapshot + quote — a facts entry, or a hedge where no open source says it).

**Implied:** one format is fine to demo (16:9); captions with the narration; the math must be
computed claims (the one equation — distance = speed x time — and the position count).

**Assumptions:** "sources for every number" extends to the satellite constellation figures
(24+ operational, ~20,200 km orbits, the speed of light's rounding); the request does not ask for
vertical — 16:9 is the demo's format (a revision adds 9:16 by re-running, not by re-asking).
