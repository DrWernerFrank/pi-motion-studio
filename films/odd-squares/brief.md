# Brief: Odd numbers make squares (≈60-80 s · 16:9 + 9:16 · narrated)

**One line:** a visual proof that 1 + 3 + 5 + … + (2n − 1) = n²: each odd number is an L-shaped gnomon
that wraps a square into the next square; the k-th L is a column of k and a row of k − 1 (2k − 1 cells,
= k² − (k − 1)²); summing the differences telescopes to n².

**Must-haves:** a moving image in the first 3 s (one cell, an L wraps it, a 2×2 square) · the squares
grow one L at a time to 5×5 · why the L is 2k − 1 (braces on its arms) · the algebraic close (telescoping
differences of squares) · recap in 3 lines with a hold.

**Color = concept (design.json math.roles):** gnomon1..gnomon5 = the 1st..5th L and its odd number,
square = n² / the square, measure = braces and counts. Every number on screen is a registered claim.
