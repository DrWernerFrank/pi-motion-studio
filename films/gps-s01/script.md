# How GPS finds you — the script

## scene s01_hook: the question

[s01.1] Your phone knows where it is, to within a few meters, using satellites twelve thousand miles overhead.
[s01.2] The trick is a clock. {shows}Every GPS satellite carries an atomic clock, and broadcasts exactly what time it is.

## scene s02_meaning: the clock trick

[s02.1] Your phone receives the signal a moment later, and measures the delay. {delay_marks}
[s02.2] The signal travels at the speed of light — a defined constant, two hundred ninety nine million seven hundred ninety two thousand four hundred fifty eight meters every second. {formula_lands}{eq_shown}
[s02.3] Distance equals that speed times the delay. {equation_lands}So one satellite gives one distance — a sphere around it. {sphere_grows}
[s02.4] Three spheres intersect in one point. {spheres_close}That is where you are. {spot_pins}
[s02.5] But your phone's clock is not atomic. {clock_gap}So a fourth satellite corrects it — solving position and time together. {fourth_draws}

## scene s03_recap: the payoff

[s03.1] Four satellites, one clock trick, and geometry: {recap_stack}that is how GPS knows where you are.
