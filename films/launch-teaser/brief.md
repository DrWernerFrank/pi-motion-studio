# launch-teaser

## The request (verbatim)

A 25-second vertical teaser for Motion Studio itself: what it feels like to say what you want and watch it get made. Use real captures of the studio's own GUI.

## Interpretation

**Goal:** 25 seconds that feel like the product: you type a sentence, the studio thinks, a film
appears — the loop made visible.

**Explicit:** 25 seconds; vertical (9:16); real captures of THIS GUI (the films list, the live
seek view, the sheets, the gates) — never redrawn.

**Implied:** the teaser is itself made by the studio (the proof is the process); captions not
required but the pacing must land on beats; one design system sampled from the GUI's own palette.

**Assumptions:** "real captures" = `studio capture` against the running GUI (localhost:3142) —
the studio's own chrome, its own films.
