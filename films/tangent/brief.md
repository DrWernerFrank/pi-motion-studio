# Brief: tangent — the derivative as the slope (≈60 s · 16:9 + 9:16 · narrated, no music)

**One line:** by the end the viewer sees the derivative as the slope of the tangent: a secant through two
points of y = x² pivots into the tangent as the gap h shrinks, a live readout on the graph shows the slope
settling on 2, the slope at every x is 2x, and the power rule (x² → 2x, x³ → 3x², xⁿ → n·xⁿ⁻¹) follows.

**Must-haves (mission P12):** secant to tangent · a live slope readout on the graph · the power rule for
n = 2 and n = 3 · recap with a hold. ≥15 claims; every number on screen from sympy (diff, limit).

**One running example:** f(x) = x² at x = 1: secant to x = 2 has slope 3; h = 1/2 → 5/2, h = 1/10 → 21/10,
the limit 2 = f'(1); then f'(2) = 4, f'(3) = 6 → f'(x) = 2x.

**Composition:** graph in panel 0 (left in landscape, top in portrait), the algebra in panel 1; the graph
never moves between scenes (seams are invisible); text never sits inside the plot (readouts live in the
lab's top row, tick labels below the axis).
