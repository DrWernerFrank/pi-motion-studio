# chart — a grown technique (K12)

animated data charts: bars racing ranks over time, drawn from a data file the film reads (every figure a sourced fact)

Craft: a chart film is built like every film in this studio — one timeline, one design system,
springs and beat-locked sound — but its content is this technique's own, so write the craft
paragraph HERE before the first segment: what the viewer must feel in the first three seconds,
what must stay true on a 360 px phone screen, what this technique can do that no other
technique can, and what it cannot do so the plan can say so honestly. Name the files the
technique owns and the gates that decide done (engine/kinds/chart/gates.mjs). When the ask
needs something this technique cannot carry, pick another and write the alternatives —
including the simplest thing that could work — into the plan with their reasons. Two hundred
characters is the floor of this paragraph, not the goal; the growth check counts them.
